Our paper [Anytime-valid goodness-of-fit test by e-spacings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23775) was published on arXiv. We bridge the gap between nonparametric and likelihood-ratio tests by using spacings to map continuous distributions under the null and alternative hypotheses to Bernoulli models. The proposed procedures provide finite-sample control of the probability of ever rejecting a true null hypothesis, even under any stopping time.
